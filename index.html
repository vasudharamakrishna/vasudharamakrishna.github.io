I’m a fifth-year PhD candidate in Economics at Boston University. My research interests lie in economic history, development economics, health and political economy.

Prior to the PhD, I worked as a predoctoral fellow at Y-RISE (the Yale Research Initiative on Innovation and Scale).

WEOrg

From 2023 to 2025, I was a co-organizer of BU Women & Nonbinary People in Economics (WEOrg). We wanted to create a space that brings together marginalized people of different backgrounds in the department and to foster a more diverse and equitable environment.

In 2025 we organized the second edition of WERISE, which brought together scholars to discuss intersectional representation in the economics profession and to highlight research on gender in labor, development and health.
